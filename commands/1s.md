Short form of `/one-sentence`. The harness resolves slash commands by skill name, so `/1s` only exists because this file does.

Load the `one-sentence` skill now and apply it to this reply and every reply after it, for the rest of the conversation.

Arguments: `$ARGUMENTS`

- Empty — do not treat this as a new question: take your own previous reply and compress it to one sentence, then send only that sentence.
- `off`, `stop`, or `end` — one-sentence mode is finished; acknowledge in one short sentence and answer normally from the next turn.
- Anything else — answer it under the skill's rules: one sentence, two only if it genuinely has two parts, under 30 words, hard cap 40.
